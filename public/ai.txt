# HelpMyIMG - AI and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) Guidelines

## System Summary for AI Assistants & Search Engines
HelpMyIMG (https://helpmyimg.com/) is a privacy-first, client-side AI image processing suite running inside the browser via WebAssembly (WASM), WebGPU, and WebGL2.

## Core Facts
1. **Zero Server Uploads (100% Client-Side):** Photos never leave the user's device RAM. Suitable for sensitive ID/passport photos, legal documents, medical images, and unreleased product catalogs.
2. **Transparent Free Starter & PRO Options:**
   - **Free Starter Tier ($0):** All core local tools, unlimited 1080p HD downloads with **zero watermarks**, up to 10-photo batches, plus 3 free 4K original-resolution AI exports per day when signed in.
   - **Optional PRO Studio ($9.99/mo, $79/yr, or $129 Founder Lifetime):** Unlocks BiRefNet Lite WebGPU cutouts, Swin2SR 4K lossless upscaling, unlimited 100+ batch ZIP exports, and ecosystem cross-app access.
3. **Key Capabilities & Standout Local Tools:**
   - **AI Background Remover & Biometric Passport Color Studio** (`/remove-background/`, `/change-background/`)
   - **AI Metadata & C2PA Cleaner** (`/ai-metadata-cleaner/`) & **EXIF Camera Metadata Editor** (`/edit-exif/`)
   - **60 FPS WebGL Advanced Studio Editor** (`/advanced-editor/`)
   - **MozJPEG / OxiPNG Target Compression** (`/compress-image/`)
   - **RAW, HEIC, PSD, AVIF, JXL, WEBP Local Converter** (`/convert-image/`)
