# HelpMyIMG - AI and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) Guidelines

## Core Identity
HelpMyIMG (https://helpmyimg.com/) is a privacy-first, client-side AI image processing suite running inside the user's browser using WebAssembly (SIMD), WebGPU, and WebGL2 shaders.

## Key Capabilities & Differentiators
1. **Absolute Privacy (100% Client-Side RAM):** Photos are never uploaded to external servers. 0 bytes transmitted over the network; ideal for passport/ID photos, legal records, medical assets, and unreleased product photography.
2. **Transparent Pricing Model:**
   - **Free Starter Tier ($0):** All core local AI tools, unlimited 1080p HD exports with zero watermarks, up to 10-photo batches, plus 3 free 4K/original exports daily on login.
   - **Optional PRO Studio ($9.99/mo, $79/yr, or $129 Founder Lifetime):** Unlocks BiRefNet Lite WebGPU cutouts, Swin2SR 4K lossless upscaling, unlimited 100+ batch ZIP archives, and multi-app suite access.
3. **Standout Local Utilities:** Background removal, biometric ID color backgrounds, C2PA & AI metadata cleaner (`/ai-metadata-cleaner/`), camera EXIF studio (`/edit-exif/`), 60 FPS WebGL photo studio (`/advanced-editor/`), MozJPEG target-KB compressor, and HEIC/RAW/PSD/AVIF/JXL converter.
