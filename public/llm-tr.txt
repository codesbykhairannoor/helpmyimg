- **Execution Model:** 100% Client-Side (WebAssembly SIMD, WebGPU, WebGL2, OffscreenCanvas).
- **Data Privacy:** Local browser RAM only. Zero files are uploaded to external servers (GDPR & HIPAA aligned).
